Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to basic questions about using our prompts on different platforms.

What is KShare?
KShare is a curated library of high-quality, hand-tested AI prompts for image generators and creative AI tools. We help creators, designers, and prompt engineers find copy-paste ready formulas to generate spectacular AI art.
Which AI image generators are supported by KShare?
Our prompts are fully compatible with leading generative AI platforms, including Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion (XL & v1.5), Bing Image Creator, Gemini, and Adobe Firefly.
Are the AI prompts on KShare free to use?
Yes, all AI prompts shared on KShare are completely free to use for both personal and commercial projects. You can copy, modify, and use them without any licensing issues or required attribution.
How do I use these prompts in Midjourney or DALL-E?
Simply browse our collection, click the "Copy" button on your desired prompt, and paste it directly into your AI generator (e.g., the `/imagine` command in Midjourney or the input field in ChatGPT/DALL-E).
Can I submit my own AI prompts?
Yes, we highly welcome contributions from prompt engineers and creators! You can reach out via our contact page or open a pull request on our GitHub repository to submit your custom prompts.
