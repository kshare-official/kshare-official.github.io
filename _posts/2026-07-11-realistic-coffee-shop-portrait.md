## 7 Tips for Cozy and Realistic Coffee Shop Portraits
1. **Control the Background Bokeh:** Use a wide aperture setting in your prompt (like `f/1.4` or `f/1.2`) to blur background noise (like baristas and espresso machines) into beautiful circles of light.
2. **Describe the Mug Details:** Specify the container (e.g., "handmade ceramic mug" or "glass cup") and the drink (e.g., "latte art with a leaf pattern") to add realistic focal points.
3. **Incorporate Window Reflections:** To add dramatic layers, include terms like "subtle reflections of passing street traffic on the window glass."
4. **Use Seasonal Wardrobe:** Dress your subject in cozy clothing like "chunky knit sweaters," "woolen scarves," or "tweed jackets" to enhance the warm, seasonal feel.
5. **Set the Time of Day:** Adjust the lighting mood by specifying the time. For example, "afternoon golden hour" creates glowing beams, while "rainy day" creates soft, moody lighting.
6. **Focus on Hand Poses:** Describe hands holding the mug, as hands warming around a hot cup is a universally recognizable gesture of comfort.
7. **Emphasize Texture Contrast:** Mention contrasting textures like "smooth porcelain mug," "rough wooden table," and "soft cashmere sweater" to build rich visual depth.

## FAQs
**Q: How do I get realistic steam in Midjourney?**  
A: Use specific terms like "delicate curls of steam rising" or "whisps of hot steam," and make sure there is a slightly darker background behind the mug so the light steam stands out.

**Q: Can I change the style to a modern, minimalist cafe?**
