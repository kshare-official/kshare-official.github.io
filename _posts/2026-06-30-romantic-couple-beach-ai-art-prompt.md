## Key Elements of a Romantic Beach Prompt
To render a high-quality romantic couple beach portrait, several variables must be controlled:
1. **Intimate Body Language & Posing:** Describe natural interactions, such as touching foreheads, holding hands, or kneeling to propose, to capture authentic chemistry.
2. **Camera & Lens Settings:** Direct the AI to simulate professional DSLR cameras (e.g., Sony Alpha 1, Canon EOS R5) paired with premium portrait lenses (e.g., 85mm f/1.2 or f/1.8) for creamy bokeh and realistic skin textures.
3. **Cinematic Lighting:** Use lighting cues like golden hour sunset, soft blue-hour moonlight, or volumetric sunlight to enhance the mood and emotional atmosphere.
4. **Wardrobe & Styling:** Detail high-fashion clothing materials (e.g., white linen shirts, flowing satin dresses, white bridal gowns) to give the scene a luxury editorial feel.
