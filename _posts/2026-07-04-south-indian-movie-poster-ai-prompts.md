## Key Elements of a South Indian Movie Poster Prompt
To render a high-quality movie poster that looks ready for the big screen, several variables must be controlled:
1. **Low-Angle Hero Shot:** Guide the AI with composition cues like "low-angle shot," "dramatic hero pose," or "looking away intensely" to establish authority and cinematic gravity.
2. **Atmospheric FX & Textures:** Use keywords like "swirling smoke," "rising embers," "flying sparks," and "dust particles" to add motion, grit, and environmental depth.
3. **High-Contrast Rim Lighting:** Focus on dramatic backlighting, rim spotlights, and color contrasts (like gold-on-black or neon red-on-blue) to make the main character pop off the background.
4. **Theatrical Painting / Film Style:** Blend realistic human details with "bold digital brushstrokes," "theatrical poster illustration," or "cinematic key art" styles to achieve a polished graphic design feel rather than a simple snapshot.

## Pro Tips for South Indian Movie Poster AI Art
1. **Leave Space for Typography:** If you intend to add title cards, credits, or taglines, add keywords like "minimalist dark sky at the top" or "smoky dark bottom" to provide clean layout margins.
2. **Experiment with the Stylize Parameter:** In Midjourney, keep the stylize value between `--stylize 150` and `--stylize 250` to encourage bold, theatrical brushstrokes rather than raw photos.
3. **Use Aspect Ratios Correctly:** Standard theatrical posters use a vertical layout. Always use `--ar 2:3` or `--ar 9:16` to ensure the composition scales correctly for standard print files and screens.
