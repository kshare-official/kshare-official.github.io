## Key Elements of a 4-Panel Collage Portrait Prompt
To generate a clean, ultra-realistic 4-panel portrait collage, structure your prompt with these essential details:
1. **Grid Layout Specification:** Explicitly define the frame arrangement using phrases like "4-panel photo collage," "clean 2x2 photo grid layout," or "four separate panel shots."
2. **Subject & Traditional Aesthetics:** Describe the model's appearance clearly, such as "an elegant Indian woman wearing a classic silk saree," "subtle gold jhumka earrings," and "natural beauty."
3. **Panel Expression Variety:** Direct expression shifts across panels, e.g., "featuring four candid expressions: warm smile, quiet gaze, side profile, and gentle laugh."
4. **Consistent Studio Lighting:** Ensure seamless color harmony using terms like "soft warm studio lighting," "consistent golden hour glow across panels," or "clean neutral background."
5. **Camera & Grid Physics:** Guide camera simulation with words like "shot on 85mm portrait lens," "crisp panel borders," "high-resolution portrait photography," and "sharp detail in each frame."

## FAQs
**Q: Why does the AI sometimes merge panels into one big image?**  
A: This occurs if the prompt lacks strong layout triggers. Use phrases like "clean 2x2 photo grid layout" and "crisp panel borders" to enforce panel separation.

**Q: Can I use different outfits in each panel?**  
A: Yes, though specifying one consistent outfit yields cleaner identity preservation across the 4 panels.

**Q: Which aspect ratio works best for 4-panel collages?**  
A: A vertical aspect ratio like `--ar 4:5` or `--ar 9:16` provides ideal spacing for balanced 2x2 squares.
