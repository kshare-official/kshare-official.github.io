1.  **Vary the Wardrobe Style:** Swap "avant-garde luxury black blazer" with "high-collar trench coat," "silk evening gown," or "edgy leather jacket" to match the style of your custom magazine.
2.  **Adjusting Lighting Moods:** Try using "soft warm golden hour glow" for a summer edition, or "cool high-contrast blue flash" for an edgy, avant-garde winter cover.
3.  **Optimize for Text Layers:** Use parameters like `--style raw` to keep the background flat and even, ensuring that text titles remain highly readable.

## FAQs
**Q: Can I add actual text (like VOGUE or GQ) using Midjourney?**  
A: Yes! Midjourney v6 is excellent at rendering short text. You can add phrases to your prompt like: `the text \"VOGUE\" in clean modern serif font overlayed on top`.

**Q: Which aspect ratio works best for cover layouts?**  
A: Standard magazine covers use vertical print formats. An aspect ratio of `--ar 3:4` or `--ar 4:5` is ideal for capturing this crop naturally.
