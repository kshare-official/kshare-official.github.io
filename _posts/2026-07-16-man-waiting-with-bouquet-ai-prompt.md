1. **Anticipatory Facial Expressions:** Explicitly guide the subject's emotional state using terms like "expression of nervous excitement," "soft smile of anticipation," "waiting patiently," or "thoughtful look."
2. **Bouquet Composition & Texture:** Describe the flowers and wrapping in detail. Use phrases like "hand-tied bouquet of fresh red roses and white baby's breath," "wrapped in textured brown kraft paper," or "delicate green leaves with tiny water droplets."
3. **Natural Hand Placement:** Ensure realistic hand anatomy by using action words like "both hands gently holding the bouquet wrapper," "cradling the flowers in his arm," or "holding a bouquet in one hand."

5. **Camera Settings & Bokeh:** Simulate professional portrait gear using terms like "shot on 85mm lens," "shallow depth of field," "soft background bokeh," "natural skin textures," and "f/1.4 aperture."

## Pro Tips for Bouquet Portraits
1. **Control the Lighting:** Specify the time of day (like "dusk," "golden hour," or "overcast morning") to establish a natural, moody atmosphere that enhances the emotional tone.
2. **Describe the Wrapping:** Instead of just "flowers," describe the wrapping material (e.g. "kraft paper," "rustic burlap," or "clear cellophane") to give the AI engine realistic textures to render.
3. **Use the Midjourney Stylize Parameter:** A stylize value between `--stylize 120` and `--stylize 180` helps keep the subject's face looking natural while allowing the background lights to melt into beautiful, artistic bokeh.
