## Key Elements of a Sunglasses Fashion Prompt
To generate an ultra-realistic sunglasses fashion portrait, structure your prompt using these five core parameters:
1. **Eyewear Style & Frame Material:** Specify frame physics clearly, e.g., "classic gold wireframe aviator sunglasses," "chunky black acetate oversized sunglasses," or "retro tortoiseshell wayfarers."
2. **Lens Tint & Reflection Physics:** Detail lens optics with terms like "dark tinted lenses with subtle golden hour sunset reflection," "mirrored chrome lenses reflecting palm trees," or "gradient tinted lenses."
3. **Model Styling & Demeanor:** Define subject posture, such as "a stylish model wearing a leather jacket," "confident side-profile glance," "relaxed summer vibe," or "high-fashion editorial stance."
4. **Cinematic Ambient Lighting:** Direct light behavior using phrases like "warm sunset rim lighting," "soft studio key light," "glowing street lamp reflections," and "high-contrast shadows."
5. **Camera & Lens Mechanics:** Simulate commercial optics with keywords like "shot on Hasselblad 100mm lens," "f/2.8 aperture," "crisp frame detail," "natural skin textures," and "8k resolution."
