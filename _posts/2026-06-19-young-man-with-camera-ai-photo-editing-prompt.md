## Pro Tips for Gemini Photo Editing
1. **Define the Camera Brand:** Mentioning brands like "Sony Alpha", "Canon EOS", or "Fujifilm" helps the AI generate precise dials and lens shapes.
2. **Control the Lighting:** Specify the exact time of day, such as "golden hour", "blue hour", or "overcast afternoon" to change the mood completely.
3. **Experiment with Wardrobe:** Change "bomber jacket" to "cozy knitted sweater" or "linen shirt" to match different seasons and aesthetics.
