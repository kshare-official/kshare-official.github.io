## Mastering Dual-Subject Realism in AI Photography
The secret to photorealistic couple portraits lies in directing camera physics and emotional intimacy simultaneously. Rather than generic instructions like "a realistic couple," modern prompt engineering specifies exact lens focal lengths (such as 85mm prime), depth of field parameters (f/1.8 aperture), skin texture details (visible pores, fine facial hair, natural skin tone variation), and atmospheric light dynamics. ## Key Elements of a Photorealistic Couple Portrait Prompt
To achieve 100% photorealistic dual-subject portraiture, structure your prompt using these five essential parameters:
1. **Subject Descriptions & Proximity:** Detail both subjects clearly, e.g., "a handsome young man and an elegant woman sitting close together," "arm gently resting on his arm," or "foreheads touching softly."
2. **Authentic Facial Expressions:** Guide emotional tone with phrases like "soft affectionate glances," "candid relaxed laughter," "warm serene smile," or "gentle loving gaze."
3. **Camera & Lens Optics:** Simulate high-end camera sensors with terms like "shot on Sony A7R V," "85mm f/1.4 prime lens," "shallow depth of field," "creamy background bokeh," and "8k resolution."
4. **Natural Light Behavior:** Specify light source dynamics, such as "soft window light falling on faces," "warm golden hour backlighting," "cinematic studio rim light," or "diffused morning sun."
5. **Photorealistic Skin & Fabric Physics:** Direct micro-details using keywords like "natural skin pore texture," "subtle freckles," "crisp fabric weave folds," and "raw portrait photography."
