## Pro Tips for Bike Lifestyle Art
1. **Define the Stance:** Specify exactly how the person is interacting with the bike (e.g., "leaning against the fuel tank", "sitting astride the seat", or "holding a helmet while standing nearby").
2. **Detail the Metals:** Use keywords like "brushed chrome", "matte black", or "polished brass" to give the AI clear texturing instructions.
3. **Set the Scene:** Roadside dust, coastal highways, and neon-lit city alleys make for the most dramatic backgrounds.
