Traditional Indian wedding photography focuses on the intricate beauty of bridal fashion. A classic combination includes an embellished red bridal lehenga with heavy gold embroidery (zari work) and a majestic groom’s sherwani. By placing these subjects in a heritage setting—like a royal palace archway decorated with marigold garlands—you create a timeless visual that reflects the luxury of a real Indian wedding.
