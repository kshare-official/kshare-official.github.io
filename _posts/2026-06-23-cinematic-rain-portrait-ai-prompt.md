## The Aesthetic of Rainy Night Portraits
A successful cinematic rain photo depends on how water interacts with light and surfaces. When rain hits a neon-lit street, it creates vibrant reflections on the wet ground. Water droplets on the subject's face, hair, and clothes catch the streetlights, adding a layer of hyper-realism. This prompt is built to guide the AI to render these micro-reflections and textures flawlessly, making the generated scene feel like a real film still.

## Why This Prompt Works
Generative AI tools can sometimes produce flat rain effects that look like simple overlays. Our prompt uses specialized photographic language—such as "anamorphic lens," "wet-surface reflections," and "water beads"—to force the model's rendering engine to integrate the moisture dynamically. This ensures that the water droplets look physical, interacting naturally with the subject's skin and clothing.

## Key Features
* **Physical Water Interaction:** Renders realistic water droplets clinging to skin, eyelashes, hair, and coat fabric.
* **Vibrant Neon Reflections:** Utilizes out-of-focus background neon signs to create colorful bokeh and wet-surface reflections.
* **Low-Key Cinematic Lighting:** Focuses on dramatic, moody side-lighting to highlight the subject and the rain path.
* **Widescreen Film Feel:** Simulates the look of an anamorphic lens with soft oval bokeh and cinematic color grading.

## Pro Tips for Rain Portraits
1. **Emphasize Neon or Headlights:** Always mention background light sources (like streetlights or shop signs) to give the water drops something to reflect.
2. **Describe Hair and Clothes as "Damp" or "Wet":** Telling the AI that hair is "wet" or clothing is "damp" changes the way the generator applies textures and glossiness.
3. **Use Cinematic Camera Keywords:** Phrases like "anamorphic lens" and "film noir aesthetic" guide the AI to apply movie-style color grading and cropping.
