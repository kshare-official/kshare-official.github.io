## 7 Secrets for Stunning South Asian AI Portraits
1. **Describe Specific Indian Textiles:** Instead of simply writing "Indian dress," use terms like "Banarasi silk," "Chanderi cotton," or " Chikankari embroidery" to get highly realistic textures.
2. **Specify Jewelry Materials:** Using terms like "oxidized silver jhumkas" or "antique gold matte necklaces" helps the AI generate sharp, defined metal textures.
3. **Leverage Warm Golden Hour:** Warm sunlight (e.g., "late afternoon golden hour") naturally complements olive and warm skin tones, creating a beautiful golden glow.
4. **Use Traditional Poses:** Direct the pose to feel natural and elegant, such as a "serene direct gaze" or a "soft profile looking slightly off-camera."
5. **Incorporate Architectural Elements:** Backdrops like "terracotta tiles," "carved wooden arches," or "stone jali windows" add rich cultural depth and visual interest.
6. **Set Correct Camera Lenses:** Specify an "85mm" or "105mm" lens to create a narrow field of view that separates the subject from the background.
7. **Control Skin Textures:** Keep the skin realistic by using phrases like "natural skin texture, visible pores, soft daylight, no skin smoothing filter."
