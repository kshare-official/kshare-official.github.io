## Key Elements of a Double Exposure Indian Woman Prompt
To achieve a clean, high-impact double exposure portrait, structure your prompt with these essential details:
1. **Primary Portrait Subject:** Clearly describe the model's profile, such as "a side-profile silhouette of an elegant Indian woman with her hair in a loose bun," or "a serene close-up of a South Asian woman."
2. **Secondary Landscape/Texture Overlay:** Detail the overlay scene, such as "seamlessly blended with a misty pine forest," "infused with blooming marigold flowers and branches," or "overlaid with golden-hour architectural arches."
3. **Clean Composition Keywords:** Direct the AI to blend rather than copy-paste. Use phrases like "double exposure photography," "double exposure effect," "double exposure technique," "seamlessly blended," and "high contrast silhouette."
4. **Lighting & Contrast Guidance:** Specify a solid, high-contrast background to help define the silhouette, like "set against a clean solid white background" or "against a deep charcoal backdrop."
5. **Technical Aesthetics:** Guide the rendering engine using camera details, such as "shot on 85mm lens," "film grain," "high-contrast lighting," and "fine-art gallery presentation."

## Pro Tips for Double Exposure Portraits
1. **Use a Solid Background:** Always instruct the generator to place the composition against a "solid white background" or "solid black background." This ensures the silhouette lines remain crisp and prevents the background from cluttering the double exposure effect.
2. **Limit Overlay Details:** Keep the secondary scene simple. Focus on one main element (like "pine branches" or "arched windows") to avoid a chaotic, messy output.
3. **Emphasize the Profile Angle:** Specifying a "side profile" or "silhouette profile" makes the double exposure effect significantly easier for the AI to process and render compared to a straight, front-facing portrait.
