4. **Historic Atmosphere & Sunlight:** Set the backdrop using phrases like "golden morning sunbeams filtering through," "Red Fort silhouette in background," or "clear blue sky with flying white doves."
5. **Camera & Optical Details:** Simulate camera optics with keywords like "shot on 85mm portrait lens," "f/1.8 aperture," "creamy background bokeh," "natural skin textures," and "8k resolution."
