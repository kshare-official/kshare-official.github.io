## Pro Tips for Flawless Face Replacement Renders
1.  **Strictly Avoid Angles:** Keep descriptions limited to "straight-on head alignment" and "looking directly at the camera" to ensure that the swapping coordinates map 1:1.
2.  **Ensure Even Lighting:** Avoid high-contrast lighting schemes like "chiaroscuro" or "dramatic side shadows" as they confuse swap blend layers, resulting in dark, smudged facial edges.
3.  **Tuning Style Weights:** Keep the stylization parameter low (`--stylize 100`) to maximize raw, photographic structural realism over artistic interpretation.
