## Key Elements of a Hyper-Realistic Fashion Editorial Prompt
To achieve a high-fashion editorial look that feels like a raw DSLR photograph, make sure to detail the following parameters:
1.  **Haute Couture Styling:** Explicitly describe the clothing and styling. Use terms like "structured avant-garde silk blazer," "pleated chiffon dress," "metallic silver puffer coat," or "oversized velvet cape" to build texture and shape.
2.  **Dramatic Lighting:** Guide the light direction and mood. Use terms like "high-contrast chiaroscuro lighting," "dramatic studio key light," "soft golden hour backlight," or "cinematic moody rim lighting."
3.  **Expression and Attitude:** Direct the model's gaze and posture, such as "confident direct gaze," "stoic expression," "dynamic runway posture," or "haunting editorial look."
4.  **Professional Camera & Lens Settings:** Emulate high-end camera gear by specifying camera models (e.g., "Hasselblad H6D-100c" or "Sony Alpha 7R V") and lenses (e.g., "85mm f/1.2 portrait lens") to generate high-resolution detail and a shallow depth of field.
