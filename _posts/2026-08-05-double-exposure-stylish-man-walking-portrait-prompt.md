## The Urban Aesthetics of Man Walking Double Exposure Art
At its core, a double exposure walking portrait tells a story of motion, reflection, and urban identity. ## Pro Tips for Double Exposure Composition
1. **Enforce Profile Angles:** Specifying a "side profile" or "walking stride in profile" gives the AI engine a clearer silhouette container than a full front-facing pose.
2. **Use Solid Backgrounds:** Always direct the prompt to place the art against a "solid white background" or "solid charcoal background" to prevent background clutter from muddying the exposure.
3. **Utilize Higher Stylize Values:** In Midjourney, setting `--stylize` between `150` and `220` encourages artistic blending between the subject and secondary landscape layers.
