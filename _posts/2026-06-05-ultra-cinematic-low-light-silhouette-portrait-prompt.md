## The Power of Cinematic Silhouettes
Silhouettes strip away distracting details, forcing the viewer to focus on form, posture, and the interplay between light and dark. When combined with a cinematic aspect ratio and color grading, the resulting images feel like stills from a blockbuster movie or an award-winning indie film.

## What Makes This Prompt Special?
Generating perfect low-light imagery with AI can sometimes result in murky or noisy images. This carefully engineered prompt uses specific lighting terminology to ensure clean, sharp silhouettes against beautifully exposed, moody backgrounds.

## Key Features
* **Dramatic Lighting:** Perfect execution of backlighting, rim lighting, and neon or sunset glows.

## Pro Tips for Cinematic Lighting
1. **Experiment with Light Sources:** Change "neon city lights" to "a dying sunset," "a solitary streetlamp," or "moonlight."
2. **Use Anamorphic Aspect Ratios:** The `--ar 21:9` ratio mimics classic cinema widescreen formats, adding to the movie-like feel.
3. **Play with Weather:** Adding elements like "heavy rain," "thick fog," or "smoke" diffuses the light beautifully and adds atmosphere.

## FAQs
**Q: Why do my silhouettes sometimes have details visible?**  
A: Ensure your prompt heavily emphasizes "pure silhouette" and "strong backlight." If details emerge, you can increase the `--stylize` parameter slightly.

**Q: Can I use this for non-human subjects?**  
A: Absolutely! This prompt works incredibly well for trees, buildings, animals, and vehicles.
