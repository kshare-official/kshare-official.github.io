## The Beauty of Contemplative Solitude
Solitary portraits strip away busy urban distractions, focusing entirely on a single subject amidst the grandeur of nature. When set in dry grass fields or flower meadows during golden hour, these images convey freedom, introspection, and scale, feeling like stills from an indie film.
