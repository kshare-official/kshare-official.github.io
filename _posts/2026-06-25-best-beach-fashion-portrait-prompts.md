## Why Beach Portraits Are Perfect for Summer
The beach offers a perfect mix of textures and lighting conditions for AI models. The transition from fine sand to wet shorelines, combined with turquoise ocean waves, provides a rich background canvas. When you add warm sunset lighting (golden hour) and wind-swept clothing, the AI is able to render beautiful shadows and dynamic motion, resulting in highly realistic lifestyle photography.

## Pro Tips for Beach AI Art
1. **Control the Wind:** Use keywords like "wind-swept hair" or "flowing dress" to give the scene a natural sense of motion.
2. **Describe Sand and Water Reflections:** Specifically asking for "wet sand reflecting the sunset" or "light reflecting off turquoise waves" helps the AI generate complex, realistic lighting.
3. **Use Fabric Details:** Materials like "linen," "crochet," and "silk" are rendered beautifully by AI, giving clothes an authentic, high-end feel.
