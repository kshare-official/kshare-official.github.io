## Pro Tips for High-Contrast Noir Art
1.  **Vary the Shadow Patterns:** Customize the lighting by specifying the shadow source, e.g., "shadows of a metal fire escape cast across the wall" or "slanted window pane shadows."
2.  **Add Selective Color Pops:** If you want a modern "Sin City" neo-noir look, try modifying the prompt to include a single color, e.g., "monochrome black and white still with selective glowing red scarf."
3.  **Adjusting Wet Surface Reflections:** Use words like "rain-slicked pavement," "puddle reflections," or "glistening brick walls" to double the impact of low-key lighting.
