## Key Elements of a Luxury Cinematic Studio Portrait Prompt
To render an ultra-realistic luxury cinematic studio portrait, your prompt should address the following elements:
1. **Dramatic Studio Lighting:** Describe professional lighting patterns like "Rembrandt lighting," "soft side-box illumination," "butterfly lighting," or "golden rim light" to create high-contrast depth.
2. **Luxury Wardrobe Materials:** Highlight high-end textures. Use terms like "haute couture velvet gown," "tailored silk blazer," "embroidered gold accents," or "flowing satin drapes."
3. **Atmospheric Depth:** Add elements that give the air three-dimensional space, such as "subtle volumetric haze," "soft smoke machine mist," or "atmospheric light rays."
4. **Medium Format Cameras:** Reference top-tier photographic gear like the "Hasselblad H6D-100c" or "Phase One XF," coupled with portrait prime lenses (e.g., "100mm f/2.2") to generate clean focus and soft background falloff.

## Pro Tips for Luxury Cinematic Studio Portraits
1. **Leverage Medium Format Gear:** Using camera names like "Hasselblad H6D" or "Phase One XF" instructs the AI to emulate high-megapixel clarity, wide dynamic range, and soft depth of field.
2. **Master the Rim Light:** Always mention a secondary light source like a "golden rim light" or "backlight flare" to separate your subject from dark studio backdrops.
3. **Use Textures for Backdrops:** Plain flat backdrops can look sterile. Specify "dark textured concrete backdrop," "mural canvas backdrop," or "soft velvet drapes" to add vintage sophistication.
