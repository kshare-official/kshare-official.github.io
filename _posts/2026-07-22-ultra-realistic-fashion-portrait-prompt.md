## The Science of Ultra-Realistic Fashion Photography in AI Art
Luxury fashion imagery is defined by high-contrast lighting, tactile fabric realism, and commanding model poses. Achieving photorealism in AI fashion requires directing how light interacts with luxury materials—such as metallic silk, tailored wool coats, distressed leather, or organza folds. Furthermore, modern AI engines require specific technical camera settings to avoid the typical "AI look." By requesting prime lens characteristics, natural skin micro-textures, controlled studio softboxes, and accurate depth of field, you allow engines like Midjourney and Flux to produce imagery that rivals professional Hasselblad or Leica camera captures.

## Key Elements of a Luxury Fashion Portrait Prompt
To render a photorealistic luxury fashion portrait, structure your prompt using these five crucial parameters:
1. **Haute Couture Wardrobe:** Detail luxury garments, such as "a tailored double-breasted black velvet blazer," "flowing metallic silk gown," or "structured high-collar trench coat."
2. **Tactile Fabric Details:** Specify material behavior using terms like "intricate silk weave texture," "crisp seam stitching," "subtle fabric sheen," and "draped folds."
3. **High-Fashion Pose & Pose Stance:** Define model posing like "confident direct camera gaze," "editorial side-profile pose," "striking high-fashion stance," or "relaxed luxury posture."
4. **Professional Studio Lighting:** Direct light setups using phrases like "softbox key light with subtle rim highlight," "dramatic chiaroscuro lighting," or "soft warm editorial studio glow."
5. **Camera & Lens Mechanics:** Specify camera parameters such as "shot on Hasselblad H6D-100c," "100mm f/2.2 portrait lens," "fine film grain," "subtle pore textures," and "8k resolution."
