## Key Elements of an Indian Couple Fashion Portrait Prompt
To render a high-quality couple portrait that looks 100% real, several variables must be controlled:
1. **Camera Gear & Lens Cues:** Guide the AI to emulate high-end professional equipment (e.g., Sony Alpha 7R V, Canon EOS R5) paired with prime lenses (e.g., 85mm or 105mm f/1.4) to achieve sharp focal points and soft background fall-off.
2. **Coordinated Wardrobe & Textures:** Specify details like "crimson red Banarasi silk", "raw silk sherwani", and "intricate gold zari embroidery" to challenge and showcase the high-fidelity rendering power of the AI.
3. **Posing & Natural Expression:** Describe realistic, confident expressions—like looking directly into the camera or sharing a gentle look—to convey authentic couple chemistry without a stiff, synthetic feel.
4. **Heritage Environment & Accessories:** Control the backdrop (e.g., "Jaipur palace archway", "heritage stone pillars") and add ethnic accessories (e.g., "Kundan jewelry", "gold pocket square") to set a luxurious, culturally authentic scene.

1. **Focus on Material Details:** Describe the fabrics in detail (e.g., "Banarasi silk", "raw silk", "velvet", "organza") to force the generator to render realistic reflections and weight.
2. **Leverage Coordinated Contrasts:** Choose colors that complement each other. Classic ethnic pairings include red and cream, emerald green and gold, or pastel peach and mint green.
3. **Fine-Tune Style Weights:** If using Midjourney, keep the stylize parameter moderate (`--stylize 100` to `--stylize 150`) to ensure the AI prioritizes photorealistic details over abstract artistic styling.
