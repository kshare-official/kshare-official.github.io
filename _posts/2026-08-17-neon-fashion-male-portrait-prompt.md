## Key Elements of a Neon Fashion Male Prompt
To generate an ultra-realistic neon male fashion portrait, structure your prompt using these five essential details:
1. **Model Appearance & Posture:** Describe the subject clearly, e.g., "a handsome young male model in a black leather jacket," "confident direct camera gaze," and "sharp jawline."
2. **Dual-Tone Neon Color Palette:** Detail neon light colors explicitly, such as "electric cyan blue key light on left face," "vibrant magenta rim lighting on right shoulder," or "amber and purple neon glow."
3. **Urban Cyberpunk Setting:** Set a gritty backdrop like "rain-slicked night alley in Tokyo," "futuristic neon street," "moody arcade entrance," or "dark studio with neon tubes."
4. **Wet Surface Reflections:** Direct light reflections using terms like "glowing neon reflections on wet asphalt," "rain droplets on leather jacket," or "subtle rim shine."
5. **Camera & Lens Optics:** Simulate high-end optics with keywords like "shot on 85mm prime lens," "f/1.8 aperture," "creamy background bokeh," "natural skin textures," and "8k resolution."
