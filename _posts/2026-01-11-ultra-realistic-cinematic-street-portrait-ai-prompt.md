## Pro Tips for Film-Grade Street Portraits
1.  **Introduce Weather Elements:** Use terms like "heavy downpour," "dense fog," or "falling snow" to double the moodiness and create beautiful light scatter.
2.  **Adjusting Urban Context:** Try changing "Tokyo street" to "New York brick alleyway" or "Parisian cafe street at dusk" to alter the architecture.
3.  **Controlling Face Realism:** Mention words like "natural expression" and "unretouched raw skin" to prevent the AI from applying smooth, plastic beauty filters.

## FAQs
**Q: How do I get high-contrast colors in this prompt?**  
A: Emphasize complementary lighting schemes, such as "warm orange candlelight contrasting with cool blue street illumination."

**Q: Does this style require a high stylize parameter in Midjourney?**  
A: No. Keeping the stylize parameter lower (`--stylize 150-250`) prevents the AI from making the image look too painterly, preserving raw camera realism instead.
