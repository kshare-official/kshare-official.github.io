## Pro Tips for Air Hostess AI Art
1.  **Balance Light Temperatures:** Combine warm ambient overhead aircraft lights with cool natural window light to give the scene a three-dimensional depth.
2.  **Focus on Small Accents:** Adding details like "metal name badge," "gold wing emblem," or "silk patterned scarf" prevents the uniform from looking generic or flat.
3.  **Specify Depth of Field:** Airplane cabins can be cluttered. Always include lens settings (e.g., "85mm f/1.2" or "shallow depth of field") to keep the background softly out of focus and draw full attention to the model.
